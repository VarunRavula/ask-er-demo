At EasyReplenish I designed and shipped Ask ER. Merchandisers at D2C fashion and jewellery brands type a question, get an answer from their live data, and can turn it into a report that arrives in their inbox every morning.

Under the hood

From a question to a scheduled report

What I built

  • A multi-step LLM pipeline (Claude) that picks tables, writes SQL and explains the result
  • "View SQL" and "Details" on every answer, so any number can be checked
  • Daily, weekly or monthly email reports from any answer

How I ran it

  • Prompt versioning and tracing in Langfuse, so every change could be compared and rolled back
  • Audits of real chat logs to catch failures that synthetic tests missed
  • Rolled out brand by brand, with in-app nudges to drive adoption
